01 — WHAT WE DO

We look at the whole picture.

A company does not exist in separate channels.

Its positioning influences its content.

Its content shapes perception.

Perception influences consideration.

Digital experience affects what happens next.

Vantix works across these layers — connecting brand, content, marketing, search, websites and conversion around the way a company actually needs to compete.

Not more activity. Better alignment between the activity and what it is supposed to achieve.
02 — OUR SERVICES

Digital capabilities connected by strategy.

01

Social Media Management

Social media is part of how a company is perceived, considered and remembered.

We manage the channel with attention to the brand, the platform, the audience and the performance of the content — from what gets published to what the data tells us to change.

Includes
Social Media Strategy · Content Creation · Publishing & Scheduling · Platform Management · Audience & Engagement Management · Performance Analysis · Competitor & Category Analysis · Content Optimisation
02

Content Strategy & Creative Direction

Effective content starts before production.

We establish what a company needs to communicate, which conversations it should participate in, how those ideas translate across platforms and what role each piece of content is expected to play.

This includes the planning and creative direction required to maintain consistency without making the brand predictable.

Includes
Content Strategy · Content Audits · Content Pillars · Messaging Frameworks · Editorial Planning · Content Calendars · Campaign Concepts · Creative Direction · Platform & Format Strategy · Creative Briefs · Content Optimisation
03

Brand Strategy & Positioning

Positioning determines how a company occupies space in its market — and what gives people a reason to distinguish it from the alternatives.

We examine the market, audience, competitive environment and existing brand to establish clearer positioning, messaging and direction.

Includes
Brand Strategy · Brand Positioning · Value Proposition · Audience Profiling · Market & Competitor Analysis · Brand Differentiation · Brand Messaging · Brand Voice · Brand Identity Direction · Brand Architecture · Brand Guidelines · Repositioning & Brand Refresh
04

Digital Marketing

Digital marketing should have a defined commercial role.

We plan and manage digital activity around the objective, audience and stage of the customer journey — connecting campaigns, channels and measurement rather than treating each activity separately.

Whether the priority is awareness, qualified traffic, leads, consideration or conversion, the execution is built around what the company needs the digital channel to accomplish.

Includes
Digital Marketing Strategy · Campaign Planning · Paid Digital Campaigns · Audience Targeting · Lead Generation · Conversion Strategy · Retargeting · Digital Customer Journeys · Campaign Optimisation · Performance Analysis · Marketing Funnel Strategy · Conversion Tracking

Local & Advanced SEO

We work across local, technical, on-page and broader organic search to strengthen visibility, relevance and the underlying structure that allows a website to perform in search.

Brand Perception & Conversion

We examine the points where perception, messaging, digital experience and customer decision-making meet — identifying friction that may be affecting consideration or conversion.

03 — HOW WE THINK

The problem is not always where it appears.

A social channel can underperform because the positioning is unclear.

Content can struggle because there is no defined role behind it.

A website can lose potential customers long before the conversion point.

Search performance can be limited by problems that have little to do with content volume.

So rather than treating every digital issue as a separate task, we look at the relationship between the moving parts.

The work starts with understanding what is happening — then determining what needs to change.
