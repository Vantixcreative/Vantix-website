03 · HOW WE THINK

The problem is not always where it appears.

A social channel can underperform because the positioning is unclear.

Content can struggle because there is no defined role behind it.

A website can lose potential customers long before the conversion point.

Search performance can be limited by problems that have little to do with content volume.

So rather than treating every digital issue as a separate task, we look at the relationship between the moving parts.

The work starts with understanding what is happening, then determining what needs to change.
